\paragraph{What the evidence supports}
The experiments show adaptation against fixed detectors and substantial suppression of several measured watermarks. They also expose limits of raw evasion: BIRA suppression accompanies lower bidirectional entailment; some DC-AE weight merges suppress detection while damaging class alignment; and probability resampling can bypass the sampler under a stronger oracle than a closed provider normally reveals.

None of these observations establishes universal watermark erasure. A negative result from one owner detector does not exclude an unknown second mark. Similarity metrics do not certify semantic equivalence or image usefulness. Preserved class alignment does not certify diversity, realism or absence of memorization.
