\section{Interpreting bypass and preservation}
A rewrite can change keyed contexts and erase statistical alignment while preserving some meaning. BIRA and ordinary rewriting use only text in the current study; their quality must be assessed independently of detection. Probability resampling generates a fresh response. When its oracle exposes pre-watermark probabilities, it bypasses the sampler; that is stronger access than a text-only provider.

Weight merging forms $\theta_\lambda=\lambda\theta_w+(1-\lambda)\theta_0$ and requires compatible clean weights. Neural functions do not interpolate linearly with their parameters. Clean training uses ordinary prediction or denoising losses and can forget a marking signal without consulting the owner detector. Output purification regenerates one image with a separate model and may alter details or framing. These are distinct attack surfaces.

A useful removal claim needs both low detection and a defined utility constraint. NLI can truncate or misclassify; cosine similarity does not certify entailment; perplexity does not certify truth. LPIPS measures paired appearance rather than population realism. Fixed qualitative sample IDs expose successes and failures without cherry-picking. No single metric certifies preservation.
