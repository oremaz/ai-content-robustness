256 independent clean calibration images. The attacker receives only
output PNGs and an independent public SD2.1 model. No target weights,
internal states or watermark-detector feedback enter the attack.

Using separately attacked calibration images to recalibrate thresholds
gives delta-40 detection of 21.5\% for RAR-XL, 35.5\% for UViT-H/DC-AE.
All attack strengths were fixed before owner scoring.

NFPA substantially weakens the measured watermarks, but does not remove
every detectable output. The low SSIM and increased LPIPS, alongside
inspected changes to faces, hands, fish details and framing, show
substantial image alteration. CLIP cosine measures broad visual
similarity and cannot certify exact identity or detail preservation.
These are raw detector-evasion rates, not quality-qualified removal
success rates.

The public SD2.1 mirror differs in hosting from the unavailable original
endpoint; its revision and weight hashes are recorded. The experiment
simulates closed access using preserved DistSeal PNGs, rather than
querying a live commercial API.
